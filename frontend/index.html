About MediDecode

Healthcare AI

Smarter Healthcare. A Healthier You.

MediDecode is an intelligent healthcare utility designed to bridge the gap between handwritten prescriptions and clear, actionable patient understanding. Using dual-pass OCR and verified pharmaceutical databases, MediDecode extracts medication schedules, flags potential drug-drug interactions, and identifies affordable generic substitutes.

How MediDecode Works

1

Image Preprocessing

OpenCV deskewing, noise filtering, and adaptive thresholding prepare the prescription image for optical character recognition.

2

Dual-Pass OCR

PaddleOCR extracts raw text and line-level confidence scores, flagging low-confidence characters for human review.

3

Database Matching

RapidFuzz entity matching validates brand names against verified local pharmaceutical catalogs.

4

Safety & Savings

NetworkX graph engine checks pairwise drug interactions and surfaces cost-effective generic alternatives.

Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, JPEG, and PNG images up to 5MB. Clear, well-lit, and in-focus images yield the highest extraction accuracy.

Are generic alternatives safe?

Yes. Verified generic substitutes contain the exact same active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), strength, and dosage form as brand-name drugs.

Does this replace medical advice?

No. MediDecode provides informational assistance only. Always consult a qualified medical professional before altering any medication regimen.
